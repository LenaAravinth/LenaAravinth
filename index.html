I'm targeting PM-1 or PM-2 roles at product companies — specifically in e-commerce, logistics tech, or SaaS. I've held a PM title before, so I'm not looking for APM roles. I'm also open to Senior BA roles that have a clear PM growth path.

Logistics, last-mile delivery, and supply chain tech. My BBA in Integrated Shipping & Logistics Management combined with 4+ years of hands-on product work in pharma retail and logistics makes this my sharpest angle — and one that's rare among PM candidates.

I'm not a developer, but I'm technical enough to work closely with engineers. I've written API test cases, worked with SQL for data analysis, collaborated on integration specs, and managed bug trackers across manual and API testing. I understand technical constraints without needing to be hand-held.
